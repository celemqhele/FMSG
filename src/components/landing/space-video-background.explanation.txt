FILE: space-video-background.tsx
PURPOSE: Full-screen video background for the landing page. Plays a space video in slow motion (0.5x) during normal viewing. When isTransitioning is true, fast-forwards (2x) for a cinematic transition effect. Uses requestAnimationFrame for smooth playback rate interpolation and gradient overlays for text readability.

IMPORTS:
- useTransition from transition-provider — reads isTransitioning state to switch between slow/fast playback
- React (useRef, useEffect, useState, useCallback) — video ref, smooth rate animation, ready state

EXPORTS:
- SpaceVideoBackground — accepts src, optional slowPlaybackRate and fastPlaybackRate props

DEPENDENCIES: Consumed by page.tsx (landing page)

SIDE EFFECTS: Starts video autoplay on mount (muted). Smoothly animates playbackRate via requestAnimationFrame.
