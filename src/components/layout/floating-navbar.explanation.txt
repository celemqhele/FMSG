FILE: floating-navbar.tsx
PURPOSE: Floating frosted-glass navigation bar for the public landing page. Uses liquid glass effect (backdrop-filter) so the space video is visible through it. Shows FMSG logo left, nav links (Home, Pricing, About) centre, Log In / Sign Up right on desktop; hamburger menu with all options on mobile. All text is white for readability over the dark video background.

IMPORTS:
- lucide-react (Menu, X) — mobile hamburger and close icons
- ../landing/liquid-glass.css — frosted glass effect styles

EXPORTS:
- FloatingNavbar — accepts onLoginClick and onSignUpClick callbacks

DEPENDENCIES: Consumed by page.tsx (landing page)

SIDE EFFECTS: None
