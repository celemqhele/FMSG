PRIVACY POLICY
FMSG AGENTS DESKTOP APPLICATION
VERSION 0.7.2 - EFFECTIVE 2026-11-03
PROVIDER: FMSG (FIND ME SOME JOBS)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SCOPE

1.1 This Privacy Policy describes how the FMSG Agents desktop application ("Application") processes and protects information on Your device and in connection with Your FMSG account.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. INFORMATION PROCESSED BY THE APPLICATION

2.1 Account and Profile Information
When You sign in with Your FMSG account, the Application reads Your account status and Your job search profiles (job titles, location preferences, industry settings, and CV details) under Your authenticated session. Your profile data is used solely to search for matching roles and tailor Your application documents.

2.2 Local Session and Saved Site Credentials
Your sign-in session and any third-party job site credentials or sensitive fields You choose to save are encrypted locally on Your computer using operating system data protection. Only the Application running under Your Windows user account can unlock this data. If You select one-time entry instead of saving, the information is used only for the current application and is not retained on disk.

2.3 Device Update Identifier
On first launch, the Application generates a random installation security key stored in non-roaming operating system protected storage. This key is used solely to authenticate update checks and decrypt software updates for Your installation. It is independent of Your user account, contains no personal information, and is never included in profile tables or data exports.

2.4 Diagnostic Logs
In standard releases, only minimal redacted error records are kept locally to recover from startup failures. Passwords, session tokens, encryption keys, saved credentials, and personal CV contents are never written in plaintext to log files.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. HOW INFORMATION IS SHARED

3.1 We do not sell Your personal information. Data is transmitted only as required to operate the service:
(a) To FMSG backend services over encrypted HTTPS connections to authenticate Your account, load Your profiles, and verify billing balances;
(b) Directly to the employer or job board websites You instruct the Application to apply to inside the visible browser window on Your computer;
(c) To the FMSG update service to check for and download verified software updates.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

4.1 You can view and update Your profile information at any time through Your FMSG account on https://findmesomejobs.co.za.

4.2 You can clear Your local session by signing out in the Application. When uninstalling the Application, the uninstaller provides an option to permanently remove all locally stored credentials, settings, and installation keys from Your computer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. UPDATES TO THIS POLICY

5.1 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as part of signed Application updates. The effective date at the top of this document indicates the latest revision.
