FILE: page.tsx
PURPOSE: Landing page (/) — public entry point. Assembles all landing sections over the space video background, manages auth modal state, and triggers video fast-forward during modal transitions.

IMPORTS:
- FloatingNavbar, Footer — layout chrome (FloatingNavbar now uses liquid glass effect + nav links)
- Hero, HowItWorks, DataPrivacy, PricingSection — content sections in liquid glass cards
- SpaceVideoBackground — full-screen space video with slow-motion/fast-forward
- AuthModal — authentication overlay with Log In / Sign Up / Verify tabs
- useTransition — controls video playback rate during auth modal open/close

EXPORTS:
- HomePage (default) — the landing page component

DEPENDENCIES: Rendered by root layout.tsx at route /

SIDE EFFECTS: Triggers startTransition() when auth modal opens, endTransition() after 800ms. Space video autoplays on mount.
